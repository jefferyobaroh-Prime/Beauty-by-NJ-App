Orders & delivery

Where do you deliver?

We deliver to your door anywhere in Lagos. We do not deliver outside Lagos yet, and there is no pickup option for now. When you check out, pick a saved address or add a new one.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery is a flat ₦1,500 across Lagos. It is free when your order reaches ₦30,000 after any promo discount. Your cart tells you how much more to add to unlock free delivery.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Most orders arrive 2 to 4 days after we confirm your order. For bank transfers, the clock starts when we confirm your payment. After you place an order, the thank-you page shows your estimated delivery dates.

How do I check on my order?

Sign in and open My orders on your profile. You will see every order with its order number, date, total, payment status and items. Keep your order number handy. It looks like NJ-261006-4F9K.

Will the rider call me?

Yes. The rider calls the phone number you gave at checkout, so please keep it reachable on delivery day. Adding a landmark in the delivery note helps a lot.

Is there a limit on how many I can buy?

You can add up to 10 of each product to your cart. If you need more, place a second order.

Can I change my address or cancel an order?

Message us as soon as you can with your order number. If we have not packed your order yet, we can change the address or cancel it. Once it is packed or on the way, it can no longer be changed.

Payments & promo codes

What payment methods can I use?

There are three ways to pay at checkout:

  • Pay now with Paystack: card, bank or USSD in a secure pop-up.
  • Bank transfer: send the money to our account, then place your order.
  • Pay on delivery: pay the rider when your order arrives.
Is it safe to pay online?

Yes. Online payments are handled by Paystack in their own secure pop-up. We never see or store your card details.

How does bank transfer work?

Choose Bank transfer at checkout and place your order. We show you our account details, the exact amount and a narration. Use your order number as the narration so we can match your payment. We start packing once the transfer is confirmed.

How does pay on delivery work?

Pay the rider in cash or by transfer when your order arrives. Please keep the exact amount ready, because riders may not always have change.

How do I use a promo code?

Enter your code in the cart and press apply. Right now we have two codes:

  • NJFAM10 gives 10% off your items.
  • WELCOME5 gives 5% off your items.

One code works per order. The discount applies to your items, not to the delivery fee.

Does my promo code affect free delivery?

Yes, a little. Free delivery is worked out after your discount. For example, if your items come to ₦31,000 and you use a 10% code, your items become ₦27,900 and delivery is ₦1,500. The cart always shows the final total before you pay.

Do I earn points when I shop?

Yes. You earn 1 point for every ₦100 you spend. Your points show on your profile page.

Returns & refunds

Can I return something?

You can return an item within 7 days of delivery if it is unopened, sealed and in its original packaging. For hygiene reasons, we cannot take back opened beauty products unless they arrived damaged or wrong.

My item arrived damaged or is not what I ordered.

We are sorry about that. Message us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos of the item and the packaging. We will replace it or refund you.

How do refunds work?

Paystack payments are refunded to the card or bank account you paid with. For bank transfer and pay on delivery orders, we send the refund to the bank account you give us. Refunds usually take 5 to 7 working days after we approve them.

I was debited but I can't see my order.

First check My orders, because it can take a moment to appear. If it is still missing, message us with the time of payment and your Paystack reference. If the payment did not go through to us, the money is returned to your account.

Products & skin advice

Are your products original?

Yes. We only stock products from the brands and their authorised distributors. We do not sell fakes or refilled items.

How do I choose the right shade?

Test on your jawline in natural daylight, not on your hand, because your hand is often a different colour from your face. Check whether you lean warm, cool or neutral, then pick the shade that disappears into your skin. If you are between two shades, go for the lighter one and adjust with a bronzer.

Should I patch test a new product?

Yes, especially if your skin is sensitive. Put a small amount behind your ear or on your inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If you see redness, itching or swelling, do not use it. If a reaction does not settle, please see a doctor. Always read the ingredients list if you have known allergies.

How should I store my products?

Keep them away from heat and direct sunlight, which are hard on creams and serums in Lagos weather. Close lids firmly, use clean hands or a spatula, and do not share products. Look for the small open-jar symbol on the pack. It shows how many months the product stays good after you open it.
